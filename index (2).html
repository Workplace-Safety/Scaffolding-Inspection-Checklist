OSHA Requirement (29 CFR 1926.451): Scaffolds must be inspected by a competent person before each work shift and after any occurrence that could affect structural integrity. Document all inspections.

1. Structural Integrity & Foundations

Base plates/mud sills on solid, level surface
Scaffold plumb, square, and level
All frames/posts securely in place (no bent/damaged components)
Braces (horizontal and diagonal) installed and secured
Scaffold tied to structure (every 4:1 ratio - height to base)
No signs of settlement, shifting, or instability

2. Platforms & Planking

Planks fully decked (no gaps greater than 1 inch)
Planks properly secured (cleated, pinned, or otherwise secured)
Platform minimum 18 inches wide (fully planked)
No cracked, split, or damaged planks
Planks extend over supports 6-12 inches (no more, no less)
Platform free of tools, debris, and unnecessary materials

3. Fall Protection & Guardrails

Top rail at 42 inches (± 3 inches) above platform
Mid-rail installed at approximately 21 inches
Toe boards at least 3.5 inches high (where required)
Guardrails on all open sides and ends (platforms 10 ft or higher)
No gaps or openings that allow person or object to fall through
Guardrails capable of withstanding 200 lbs force (rigid, no damage)

4. Access & Egress

Safe access provided (ladder, stair tower, or ramp)
Ladder secured and extends 3 ft above platform
No cross-bracing used as access (climbing on braces prohibited)
Access point clear of obstructions

5. Overhead Protection & Clearances

Overhead protection provided where falling object hazards exist
Minimum 10 ft clearance from power lines (or de-energized)
Barricades/warning tape if scaffold over walkways or work areas
Scaffold tagged with load rating and inspection status
⚠ TAG OUT SCAFFOLD if any critical deficiency is found. Attach "DO NOT USE" tag and notify supervisor immediately.
